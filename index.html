Don’t Purchase Any Package from Trek Shrek

I am sharing this review based on our group’s personal experience with Trek Shrek, because I believe other travellers should know what happened to us before booking a trekking or travel package with them.

A group of six of us planned a Tungnath trip through Trek Shrek from 1 October to 5 October 2026. The agreed package price was ₹3,500 per person, and we made the advance payment around 15 days before the trip after finalising our booking. Since this was planned specifically around the long weekend, we had made our travel arrangements and kept those dates reserved for the trip.

Our pickup was scheduled for 10:30 PM on 1 October 2026. However, while we were already on our way to the pickup point, at around 10:00 PM, we received a call from Trek Shrek informing us that the trip had been cancelled. This was extremely disappointing because we were informed about the cancellation only shortly before the scheduled pickup time.

According to the representatives we spoke with, Suraj and Tanya, the trip had been cancelled and our advance payment would be returned. For us, however, the problem was much bigger than simply getting our money back. We had planned this trip approximately two weeks in advance, arranged our schedules around the long weekend, and were already travelling towards the pickup point when we were informed that the trip was cancelled.

We were subsequently given the impression that the cancellation was connected to the fact that there was significant demand for the trip on that date and that other people were willing to pay more. From our perspective, it felt as though our confirmed booking was not honoured because the same trip could potentially be sold at a higher price. While looking into fraud track shrek news and similar customer experiences, I cannot independently verify the company's internal reasons, but this was how the situation was communicated to us and how the experience felt from our side.

The refund was another frustrating part of the experience. We were initially told that our advance payment would be returned, but despite repeated calls and follow-ups, we did not receive the refund immediately. The amount was eventually returned on 3 October 2026, after we repeatedly contacted them.

The entire experience was particularly disappointing because our destination was Tungnath, and this was not a last-minute casual plan. We had specifically planned the trip for our long weekend and had trusted Trek Shrek after making the advance payment well in advance.

Based on our experience, I would strongly recommend that travellers do thorough research before booking any package with Trek Shrek. Do not rely only on online promotions, social media posts, or attractive package prices. Before making an advance payment, confirm the cancellation policy, refund timeline, booking confirmation, and what happens if the operator cancels the trip at the last minute.
